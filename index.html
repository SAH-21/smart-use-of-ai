PRACTICAL DEMO

Build a simple Student Tracker

01

Create the spreadsheet

Open Google Sheets → Blank Spreadsheet → name it Student Tracker.

02

Add your columns

Subject, Assignment, Due Date, Status, Score (%)

03

Add sample data

Add sample information for five subjects and update the status and scores.

04

Use formulas

=AVERAGE(E2:E6) =COUNTIF(D2:D6,"Done")
05

Visualize the data

Select the data → Insert → Chart → choose a Bar or Pie chart.

06

Ask Gemini

Use the Gemini sidebar and ask: “Which subject needs most attention?”
